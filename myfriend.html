My Friend

 

ou're an angel here on earth

You're willing to share not just our mirth

But through all my troubles sorrows and pain

You're here for me again and again.

When life seemed too hard and I felt so alone

I knew I couldn't survive all on my own.

I prayed to God, please help me cope,

He answered with you,my angel of hope.

You're patient and willing to stay by my side

From you, there's nothing I have to hide.

You're so caring,loving, gentle and kind

I know I can say what's on my mind.

I know that whatever may come my way

With you as my friend I can survive every day.

I now offer thanks to God above

For giving me an angel of love.

I thank you too for being a great friend,

I know our friendship will never end.

 

Thank youfor being such an angel.

 

Please sign my guestbook

and let me know you were here.
