Sunset & Sunrise

I've been sitting at this desk nearly all day
Finding the internet changed my life in every way.
I glance out my window and get such a fright
The sun's set again, it's suddenly night.
I tell myself I won't stay here long
I'm off to my bed where I belong.
Just checking to see who's online
I'm in control, I will be fine
I log on and there I see
Some of my friends waiting for me.
I'll have a talk, it's not too late
How are you going, I ask a mate.
And so it goes on for only a while
Each one of my friends bringing a smile.
What's happened, it can't be before my eyes
I'm witnessing yet another sunrise..
Another night 's sleep, lost in the company of friends
I really must do something, this has to end.
Tonight will be different, I predict it
Because NO.....I AM NOT ADDICTED..
