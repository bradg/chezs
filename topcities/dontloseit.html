When you find that special someone who seems to understand 
Is always there for you and takes you by the hand.
Who comforts you till the crying's all done
And helps lift you up when you're feeling down.
Your problems, this friend is willing to share
You know in your heart they really do care.


Cherish this friendship with all of your might
Don't lose it, don't risk it, it's well worth the fight.
Embrace it, remember it's a gift from above
Given to you with God's endless love.
Be open and honest and take some pleasure
In knowing you've met one of God's special treasures.
