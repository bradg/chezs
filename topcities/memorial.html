In 1999 we were saddened to lose two of our loving companions within months of each other. Freckles went to sleep peacefully with his family around him here on Valentine's Day, and then in September Baxter said goodbye and went to sleep also peacefully at home with his family here.
I remind myself daily that they are no longer in pain, and are angels looking down on us and helping us continue to love and cherish their sisters and brother.

One day in May 1983 we saw Freckles on the side of a busy road. He was so skinny and had sores on him. The vet said he was only a few months old, and was suffering malnutrition.  He had won our hearts and we were rewarded with many years of love and devotion. He loved people and going for rides in the car, but his favourite was playing ball. Even when he started to lose his sight due to cataracts he would still love a game of ball.  He would often fall asleep with one beside him, his own security blanket. His arthritis slowed him down a lot, but he still loved a game of ball. He was a fighter and loved life. until that very hot day in February when,  wrapped in a cool damp towel, he said his goodbye to us and went to sleep. 


 
 
 


 

In December of 1983 we got Baxter from a friend. He was just a pup, and a great playmate for Freckles. He wasn't into playing ball as much, and would often hide the ball and sit back and watch Freckles looking for it. LOL.I'm sure he was smiling. He loved being brushed altho to look at his pics you wouldn't think he ever was. He also loved to roll in the grass especially after a bath. He was very protective of the family and certainly let us know if anyone was at the door. He also got arthritis and sometimes had trouble getting up onto a lounge. He liked the bed we made for him on the floor with cushions to keep him warm and comfortable. His own pain from the arthritis plus the grief of losing his best mate became too much for him and in September he said goodbye and went to sleep for the last time with us here.


 
 
 




Both these wonderful friends we were given are greatly missed, but I know they are free from pain now, watching over us and waiting for us at Rainbow Bridge. We still have many memories to hold on to till we see them again.
