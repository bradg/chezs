It's Christmas time...time to have fun
Under the hot Australian sun.
Now Christmas Eve can  cause  some planning and thought
Just how do we get our little ones to bed as they ought..
With excitement, and heat, and daylight till late
It seems like Santa will just have to wait..
Finally it happens, all's quiet,  not a peep
The little darlings are all fast asleep...
Come along Santa, it's now your call
As I bid a (Yawns)  weary Goodnight to you all..


 

It's Christmas morning,  what now... do you ask?
For some it's to the beach, in the sun to bask.
Others will find that a swim in their pool
Is a very good way of staying cool.
Ahh for me, it's in my home I prefer to stay
With air conditioner and fans working all day.
Looking  around at my family with pride and love
And giving thanks to the Good Lord above.


 
 

What's that did you ask? What do we eat?
Well for some the traditonal roast is too much in our heat.
Seafoods and salads packed as picnics suffice
Or a barbeque with drinks on ice.
Some even think of an easier way
Come on now let's get take away.
Spare a thought for the girl who serves you in holiday hat
"Merry Christmas" she says, "Would you like fries with that?"


 

I hope you've enjoyed seeing a few of the ways
We in Australia celebrate this most wonderful of days.
Please take my prayer for you,  that God will Bless
You and those you love with His Peace and Happiness.
