RECIPE FOR LOVE

 

Take a heartful of warmth,

And love and kisses,

Sift in your hopes, your dreams,

And wishes,

Blend them together with laughter and tears,

Add a dash of forgiveness,

And stir through the years.

Sprinkle with kindness,

And serve generously,

With plenty of hugs,

And TLC.
