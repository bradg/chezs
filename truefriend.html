A True Friend.

A true friend is so very hard to find.

When I met you, I knew I'd found mine.

You're always here when I need a friend,

Our forever friendship will never end.

We share some fun times, but it's when it gets tough,

That you take my hand and help me through the rough.

When I'm feeling down, I can rely on you

To be here and help see me through.

 

It wasn't by luck or just out of the blue,

That I met someone as special as you.

It was meant to be, of that I am sure

With you by my side I could want for no more.

I hope and pray that when the needs be

I can be as good a friend to you as you are to me.

Although we might live miles apart,

You are always here right in my heart.

 

I may not be wealthy and have money untold

The riches I have are worth more than gold.

I became rich the day I met you

And I want you to know, you're a friend ever true.

I'll treasure our friendship right to the end

And I'm happy to say we are very

Best Friends
